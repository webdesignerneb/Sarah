Why it doesn't turn your skin green

14k gold-plated over 925 silver.

Most dropshipped jewelry is brass with a thin flash coating that fades in weeks. We plate real 14k gold over solid 925 sterling silver at a thickness that survives sweat, seawater, and sleep — so what you order in the photo is what you're still wearing in a year.
